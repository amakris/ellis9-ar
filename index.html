House in AR

Shell with MVHR and fan coils, 1:1. Pick a floor, wait for the 3D view, then tap "View in AR".

loading…

  1. Stand on the bare slab of the floor you opened; move the phone until it finds the floor.
  2. The red pole marks the outer SW corner of the SW column (same column on every floor).
  3. Drag / twist until the pole sits on that corner, the red bar along the column's S face (east) and the blue bar along its W face (north).
  4. Expect 10–30 cm drift; re-align on each floor.

The 3D view opens standing on the stair landing (top of the stair from below; basement: foot of the stair); drag to look around, pinch out to leave. Off site: "Ceiling off" removes the slab above (look in from above, or drag up close in the 3D view); "Free size in AR" lets you pinch to shrink / enlarge the model. Leave both off on site for the true 1:1 model. Needs Chrome on Android with "Google Play Services for AR" installed (Play Store). Open this page in Chrome itself, not inside another app's browser.
