House in AR

House shell with MVHR (Sky B, option D) and the fan coils, at 1:1. One file per floor, cut from that floor's bare slab to the slab above.

Lining it up on site

  1. Stand on the bare slab of the floor you opened and let the phone find the floor.
  2. Find the red pole: it marks the outer south-west corner of the south-west column (GF-COL03, same column on every floor).
  3. Drag and twist the model until the pole sits on that real corner, the red bar runs east along the column's south face and the blue bar runs north along its west face.
  4. Expect 10–30 cm drift across the house. Re-align when you move to another floor.

"Open in AR" uses Google Play Services for AR; "Google app" opens the same viewer through the Google app.
